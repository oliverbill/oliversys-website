## About

Brightember provides independent technical due diligence for European venture capital investments and M&A transactions.

We help deal teams understand whether a target’s product, engineering organization, architecture, and delivery capability can carry the investment thesis—or create exposure beneath it. Our work turns technical evidence into a clear transaction view: what is material, what is manageable, and what should change in the deal or post-close plan.

For seed and Series A investors without an in-house technical function, Brightember acts as a specialist extension of the deal team. We challenge the assumptions behind scale, product delivery, technology cost, team capability, and critical dependencies, then present the findings in language an investment committee can use.

For M&A counsel, we provide a controlled technical workstream that complements legal diligence. Findings are traceable, prioritized by transaction consequence, and structured to support clear communication with the client and wider adviser group.

Every review is scoped around the decision in front of the deal team—not a generic checklist. Typical outputs include an executive risk map, evidence-backed findings, transaction implications, and practical post-close priorities.

Brightember is based in Portugal and supports transactions across Europe. For a confidential introductory conversation, contact **contact@brightember.pt**.

## Service description

### Technical due diligence reports

> **A decision-ready technical view for investment and M&A transactions.**

Brightember reviews the evidence behind a target’s product, engineering organization, architecture, data, delivery model, and technology plan. The report distinguishes manageable technical debt from structural risk, connects findings to transaction consequences, and identifies practical priorities for the first 100 days.

The review can support venture capital investors, acquirers, and M&A counsel when technology is material to value, scalability, execution, integration, or future cost.
